Pricing questions
Is VAT included?
Yes. EGP 990 is what leaves your account, and you get a tax invoice in the dashboard every month.
What happens after the 14 days?
We ask for payment before anything publishes. Your units stay saved either way.
Can I pause during Ramadan?
You can pause for up to two months a year. Your site shows a simple contact page while paused.
Do you take a commission on deals?
Never. Aqarly is a subscription. Your deals and your leads are entirely yours.
