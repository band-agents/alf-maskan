Do I need a developer or a domain first?

No. You get a free address like kamal-estates.alfmaskan.com the moment you sign up. If you already own a domain, connect it in settings — we handle the certificate, and our team will do it for you for EGP 450 once.

Can I move my units from Property Finder or OLX?

Yes. Export your listings to a spreadsheet and import the file in one step — zone, price, area, finishing and payment plan map to Alf Maskan fields automatically. Anything we cannot match is listed for you to fix before it publishes.

Is the Arabic version a real RTL layout?

It is. The whole layout mirrors — navigation, cards, forms and icons all flip. Numbers, phone numbers and your logo stay the right way round, because those never mirror in Arabic.

How do I pay? Do you take Instapay or Vodafone Cash?

Card, Instapay and Vodafone Cash all work, billed in Egyptian pounds with VAT included. You get a tax invoice in the dashboard every month.

What happens to my site if I stop paying?

Your site shows a simple contact page instead of your listings, and every unit, photo and lead stays saved. Pay again and it is back exactly as you left it. You can also pause for up to two months a year.
