? What is DNS, in plain words

A domain is an address, not a place. Buying kamalestates.com from a registrar is like registering an address at the post office — it does not yet say which building to deliver to.

DNS is the forwarding instruction. You log in where you bought the domain, paste two lines we give you, and from then on anyone typing your address arrives at your storefront. It usually takes an hour or two, sometimes a day.
